Current Month’s Read

The Epic of Gilgamesh (ملحمة جلجامش)

Author: Ancient Mesopotamian Tradition (Sin-liqe-unninni) • Translators: Andrew George (EN) / Taha Baqir – طه باقر (AR)

Named after our club’s emblem, this month’s special anniversary reading compares Andrew George’s critical English translation with Taha Baqir’s classic Arabic translation from the original cuneiform tablets, exploring mortality, friendship between Gilgamesh and Enkidu, and the healer’s search for meaning.

Reading Target for Meetup: Tablets I – XI (Bilingual reading guide available in the club channel).
