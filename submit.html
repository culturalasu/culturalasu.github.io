📝 Share Your Voice in Arabic or English

Submit Your Work

Contribute poetry, essays, short stories, book reviews, photography, or visual designs to the Culture & Media Committee Zine and Gallery.

✔ Editorial Review Process

All submitted works are reviewed by the Culture & Media Committee admin and editorial team before publishing on the Creative Zine or Media Gallery. You will receive a confirmation within 5–7 days.

🌐 Bilingual Works (AR / EN)

We welcome original works in both Arabic (الفصحى أو العامية المصرية الراقية) and English. Written pieces should be between 150 and 2,500 words; visual works should be high-resolution PNG/JPG.

🔒 Secure Google Form Upload

To keep our static site fast and secure without server-side file storage, all manuscript attachments and file uploads are handled directly through our official Google Form below.
